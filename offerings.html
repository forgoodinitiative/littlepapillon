Our Offerings

“Through play, children learn to express themselves, build confidence, and explore the world around them.”

🍲

Daily Homemade Hot Lunch

Healthy eating is an important part of every child’s growth and development. That’s why our program offers fresh, homemade hot lunches every day. Meals are prepared with care, using fresh ingredients and a healthy balance of nutrients.

Our menu changes daily and can be viewed in the daycare or in our parent app. All our meat and chicken are Halal, and we also offer vegetarian meal options for families who prefer them.

By sharing the same healthy meals together, children enjoy a warm, family-style dining experience that encourages social connection, good manners, and healthy eating habits. This approach also helps children move away from processed and fast foods, developing a lifelong appreciation for nutritious, homemade meals.

🍎

Morning & Afternoon Snacks

Along with our daily hot lunch, we provide two healthy snacks each day — one in the morning and one in the afternoon. All snacks are freshly prepared and designed to give children the energy they need to play, learn, and grow.

Our snacks are always healthy and balanced, including a mix of fresh fruits and healthy carbohydrates such as whole-grain crackers, cereal, or baked goods. We avoid overly sweet or processed foods — sometimes offering lightly sweet options, and often completely sugar-free choices.

By encouraging healthy snacking habits, children learn to enjoy natural flavours and make better food choices as they grow.

🎨

Daily Art & Activities

Each day, children enjoy a fun and creative art or activity session, usually held in the morning. These activities help children express themselves, explore new ideas, and develop fine motor skills while having fun with friends.

Our art and activity plans are connected to the seasonal themes and celebrations in our calendar, helping children learn about the world around them in meaningful and engaging ways.

Through painting, crafting, sensory play, and hands-on learning experiences, children build confidence, creativity, and a sense of pride in their work — all while enjoying a warm and supportive environment.

🌳

Outdoor and Indoor Play & Physical Activity

Our daycare offers a spacious outdoor play area that exceeds licensing space requirements, giving children plenty of room to explore, move, and play safely. The area includes a large sandbox and a variety of equipment to support sensory development and gross motor skills.

Living in Coquitlam, where rainy days are common, we also provide indoor activity options so children can stay active and enjoy physical play even when outdoor play isn’t possible. These spaces encourage movement, coordination, and energy, ensuring children remain healthy and engaged every day.

Through a combination of outdoor and indoor activities, children develop strength, balance, and gross motor skills while enjoying a fun and safe environment.
