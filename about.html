About Us

Welcome to Little Papillon Childcare — a warm, caring, and learning through play environment where children feel safe, respected, and excited to learn.

Our program is built on a clear philosophy: children learn best through playful and meaningful experiences. We believe in learning through play, not only free play but also thoughtfully planned activities that support children’s development in language, early math, social skills, creativity, and confidence — all in a joyful and pressure-free way.

The daycare is led by an experienced early childhood educator with hands-on experience working in daycare settings. This professional experience, combined with real-life parenting, has shaped a deep understanding of children’s emotional, social, and developmental needs. We do not believe in pushing children in a school-like way. Instead, we focus on making learning fun, engaging, and age-appropriate so children develop a natural love for learning from an early age.

Our program focuses on creating rich language experiences through daily conversations, songs, stories, and interactive activities that support children’s communication skills in a natural and engaging way.

Our daily routine supports children’s growth in thinking skills, communication, independence, creativity, and self-esteem. Circle time and group activities are designed to encourage participation while respecting each child’s comfort level. We value inclusion, kindness, and respect, ensuring that every child feels seen, heard, and valued.

Our goal is to create a safe, nurturing, and inspiring space where children are happy to come each day — a place where curiosity is encouraged, friendships are built, and learning happens naturally because children want to learn.
